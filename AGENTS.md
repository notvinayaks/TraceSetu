## Mandatory product standards

For the complete self-contained project handover, exact problem statement, current implementation, research/source register, contracts, demo and deferred backlog, use `skills/custody-atlas-sih/SKILL.md`. Read its current-state overview first and retrieve only the relevant deeper sections; the embedded original research report is a historical proposal, not current implementation status. Update the skill when material facts change.

- Differentiation must improve an investigator's outcome and have a reproducible acceptance test. Graphs, multi-chain support, risk scores, case management and legal-request packaging already exist in other products; do not call those alone novel.
- Treat proposed differentiators as hypotheses until implemented and evaluated. Do not claim world-first status, superior accuracy, guaranteed recovery, official MHA endorsement, or production readiness without evidence.
- Never fabricate wallet ownership, exchange labels, transaction paths, confidence percentages, successful API calls, frozen balances or SAHYOG connectivity.
- Distinguish observed chain facts, provider assertions, investigator hypotheses, synthetic fixtures, missing coverage and independently verified service confirmations in both code and UI.
- Live mode must never silently fall back to synthetic data. No label means unknown, not proven self-custody or low risk.
- Identify the first evidenced custodial boundary per path. Do not trace across an exchange's internal customer ledger by linking unrelated withdrawals.
- Preserve exact native amounts, chronology, chain-qualified identities, source evidence and reorganisation handling. Keep amount allocation assumptions separate from actual transfers.
- Every consequential request requires an authenticated user, the appropriate agency/case permission, a verified recipient and payload-bound review. Do not send real notices or messages without explicit session authorisation.
- Public data and permitted self-hosted/open-source components are practical cost-saving options. Do not bypass paywalls, rate limits, authentication or licensing, and do not infer customer identities from deposit addresses.
- Keep a status ledger of implemented, tested, proposed and externally blocked capabilities. A working independent installation does not establish official integration or production-grade attribution.
- Maintain `IMPLEMENTATION_CHECKLIST.md` after each verified milestone, as explicitly requested by the user. Check off only the narrowly stated task after its acceptance evidence passes; preserve remaining scope and external gates. Run `scripts/update_checklist.py` to update counts. Counts are unweighted tasks, never an invented readiness percentage.
