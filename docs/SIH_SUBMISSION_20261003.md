## Evaluator criteria

Novelty is framed as the bounded first-custody/gap certificate, evidence-guided next-query planning and source-challenge/recompute workflow. Temporal graphs and clustering are established prior art; superiority and scientific uniqueness remain to be evaluated. Case CRUD and graph display are not presented as the contribution.

The 36-hour schedule is an engineering plan on the existing prototype, not a measured completion time or a promise of national deployment from scratch. It covers one bounded Bitcoin investigation, failure handling, source review and verifiable export, alongside the existing research demonstration. Other chains and independent field validation are staged.

Ministry practicality is a deployment path: separate API/worker capacity, PostgreSQL queue claims, bounded searches, caching and case permissions exist in the foundation. Shared quotas, agency fairness, SSO/MFA, load/fault validation, governed data access and official SAHYOG remain open. Public/free APIs do not establish national coverage.

The technical slide explains the purpose of FastAPI/Python, React/TypeScript/Cytoscape, PostgreSQL/workers, SQLite for the local demo, and SHA-256/Ed25519 for evidence integrity. Neither cryptographic integrity nor a modern framework establishes ownership accuracy. [FastAPI deployment concepts](https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/deployment/concepts/) and [PostgreSQL queue-lock semantics](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/sql-select.html) were checked as primary technical references.

## Research grounding

- [Victor, FC 2020](https://www.ifca.ai/fc20/preproceedings/31.pdf): deposit forwarding as a seeded heuristic, not proof that every sender is an exchange-controlled address.
- [Brechlin et al., 2025](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/nem.2325): Evonax-specific collection/fee-funding behaviour, with service-side records used to confirm roles. This does not establish a universal public-graph ownership classifier.
- [Wu et al., PVLDB 2014](https://www.vldb.org/pvldb/vol7/p721-wu.pdf): temporal paths; the project adapts chronological search to bounded transfer connectivity.
- [Kappos et al., USENIX Security 2022](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/sec22-kappos.pdf): a Bitcoin clustering extension for future guarded implementation.
- [Lubbertsen et al., USENIX Security 2025](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/usenixsecurity25-lubbertsen.pdf): independent ground truth and address-role evaluation.

The 64 synthetic reference checks were rerun and passed on 3 October 2026. This is functional/adversarial test evidence, not a real-world precision estimate. The previously recorded public Bitcoin sample established transfers, not a receiving VASP. Six parser adapters do not establish equal provider access or label coverage. SAHYOG is not connected; requests remain exported and NOT_SENT.
